How to use

  1. Spray directly onto the surface or onto a clean cloth.
  2. Wipe with a clean, dry, lint-free or microfibre cloth.
  3. For heavily soiled areas, reapply if necessary.

Ideal applications

  • Windows and glass surfaces
  • Mirrors
  • Showcases and display cabinets
  • Office partitions and glass doors
  • Stainless steel and chrome
  • Plastic and laminated surfaces

Not for use on

  • Unsealed wood
  • Alkali-sensitive surfaces
  • Electronic screens

Caution: May cause mild eye irritation. Wear gloves if used frequently and use in a well-ventilated area. Store at 5–35 °C, away from direct sunlight.
