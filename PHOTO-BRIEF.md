# Photo brief — 38 products

30 of 68 products are photographed (September 2026 batch included). The other 38
currently show a generated Swift placeholder card, which is honest but reads as
"not stocked" to a buyer. This is the list, in the order worth shooting.

Everything here goes through the same pipeline as the last batch: drop the files
in a folder, and each becomes a catalogue photo, a hero cut-out and a rail
thumbnail automatically.

---

## What to supply per product

**One shot is enough. Two is better.**

| | Purpose | What it needs |
|---|---|---|
| **A — Pack shot** | catalogue card, product page | The product, lit, sharp, filling most of the frame |
| **B — Cut-out source** *(optional)* | hero conveyor, industry rails | Same product on a **plain, evenly-lit background** — white, light grey, or any flat colour that is not on the label |

If only one is supplied, make it **B** — a plain-background shot works for both,
because a cut-out can be lifted from it and the pack shot composed around it.

### Technical

- **Minimum 1500px on the long edge.** Bigger is fine; the pipeline downsamples.
  Send the original file, not a WhatsApp copy — WhatsApp re-compresses to about
  1100–1600px, which is why four of the last batch came in under the minimum.
- **JPEG or PNG.** Phone photos are acceptable *if* the lighting is even and the
  background is plain.
- **Whole product in frame**, including the cap and the base. Nothing cropped.
- **Label facing camera and legible.** The label carries the pack size and the
  product name, which is where those values come from.
- **No hands, no props, no background clutter.** A sheet of white paper or a
  plain wall is enough.
- **Even light, no hard shadow across the label.**
- **Real packaging, not an edited label.** One image in the last batch (Bowl
  Shine in a bathroom) is generated artwork whose small print is garbled — at
  product-page size "VISTEC" and "Frece thoroughly" are readable. A real photo
  of the jerrican would replace it.

### Naming

Name the file after the product as it appears below — `oven grill cleaner.jpg`,
`pool chlorine.jpg`. Spaces and capitals are fine.
