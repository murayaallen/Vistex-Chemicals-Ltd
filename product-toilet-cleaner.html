Toilet Cleaner

Acidic toilet & bathroom descaler

Thickened phosphoric-acid cleaner that removes lime scale, hard-water deposits, uric scale, rust staining and general bowl soil. It clings to vertical surfaces for longer contact, so it works under the rim rather than running straight to the water line.

How to use

  1. Toilets: flush, then apply under the rim and onto the inside surfaces.
  2. Spread with a toilet brush and leave for 3–5 minutes.
  3. Scrub thoroughly and flush with plenty of water.
  4. Urinals: apply, leave 2–5 minutes, scrub and rinse with plenty of water.

Not for use on

  • Acid-sensitive metals
  • Damaged or unsealed surfaces

Warning / Danger: Acid cleaner. Mixing with chlorine products can release hazardous chlorine-containing gas. Wear chemical-resistant gloves and eye protection and use in a well-ventilated area.
